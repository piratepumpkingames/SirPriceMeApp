# Google Play store listings

Copy each block into **Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing → Manage translations**.

Character limits: **short description ≤ 80**, title ≤ 30.

Feature graphics (1024×500): `assets/play-store/play-feature-graphic-{sl,en,hr,de}-1024x500.png`

Optional: if you change taglines or replace `assets/sirpriceme-feature-source.png`, rerun `node scripts/generate-play-feature-graphics.mjs` to rebuild the PNGs. Once uploaded to Play Console, you do not need this for normal use.

## Slovenian (sl) — default

**Title:** SirPriceMe

**Short description:**
```
Fotografiraj predmet — AI ocena cene, domači katalog in pomoč pri prodaji.
```

**Full description:**
```
SirPriceMe vam pomaga oceniti vrednost rabljenih predmetov in jih lažje prodati — od enega kavča do celotnega podstrešja.

Kako deluje?
1. Fotografirajte predmet s telefonom.
2. Umetna inteligenca predlaga ime, stanje in oceno cene v EUR.
3. Odprete podobne oglase na Bolhi, Facebook Marketplace in drugih straneh.
4. Po želji predmet shranite v domači katalog po prostorih ali pripravite besedilo oglasa.

Kaj lahko počnete?

• AI analiza fotografije — hitra ocena cene in pojasnilo
• Povezave na lokalne tržnice — iskanje podobnih oglasov (Bolha, Facebook …)
• Pomoč pri oglasu — predlog naslova in opisa za copy-paste
• Domači katalog — predmeti po prostorih (dnevna, kuhinja, podstrešje …)
• Več fotografij na predmet, črtna koda in podatki za zavarovalnico (serijska številka, model)
• Izvoz kataloga v PDF — za selitev, pregled doma ali osebno evidenco
• Deljenje in shranjevanje fotografij predmetov ob prodaji
• Jezik vsebine: slovenščina, hrvaščina, nemščina, angleščina

Pomembno
Ocene so informativne in jih ustvarja umetna inteligenca. Niso uradna cenitev ali appraisal. Pred zavarovalnico, davčno ali pravno uporabo vrednosti vedno preverite pri pristojni osebi. Podatke kataloga hrani aplikacija na vaši napravi.

SirPriceMe — manj ugibanja, hitreje do cene in oglasa.
```

## English (en-US)

**Title:** SirPriceMe

**Short description:**
```
Snap a photo — AI price estimate, home catalog, and help selling items.
```

**Full description:**
```
SirPriceMe helps you estimate the value of used items and sell them faster — from a single couch to an entire attic.

How it works
1. Take a photo of the item with your phone.
2. AI suggests a name, condition, and price estimate in EUR.
3. Open similar listings on local marketplaces and Facebook Marketplace.
4. Optionally save the item to your home catalog by room, or generate listing text to copy and paste.

What you can do

• AI photo analysis — quick price estimate and explanation
• Links to local marketplaces — find similar listings (eBay, Facebook, and more)
• Listing assistant — suggested title and description ready to copy
• Home catalog — organize items by room (living room, kitchen, attic …)
• Multiple photos per item, barcode scan, and insurance details (serial number, model)
• Export catalog to PDF — for moving, home inventory, or personal records
• Share and save item photos when selling
• Content language: English, Slovenian, Croatian, German

Important
Estimates are informational and generated by AI. They are not official appraisals. Before insurance, tax, or legal use, always verify values with a qualified professional. Catalog data is stored on your device.

SirPriceMe — less guessing, faster to a price and a listing.
```

**Title:** SirPriceMe

```
Foto vom Gegenstand — KI-Preisschätzung, Hauskatalog und Hilfe beim Verkauf.
```

**Full description:**
```
SirPriceMe hilft Ihnen, den Wert gebrauchter Gegenstände einzuschätzen und sie schneller zu verkaufen — vom einzelnen Sofa bis zum ganzen Dachboden.

1. Fotografieren Sie den Gegenstand mit Ihrem Handy.
2. KI schlägt Name, Zustand und Preisschätzung in EUR vor.
```
